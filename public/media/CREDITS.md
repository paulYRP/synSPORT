# Opening media

`throw.mp4` adapts **JudoVideo.net: Okuri-ashi-barai**, by **canaljudovideo**, 25 November 2013.

- Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:JudoVideo.net_Okuri-ashi-barai.webm
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
- Changes: crop of frames 144–218, foreground extraction, monochrome treatment, packed colour and transparency, charcoal shading, camera zoom and scroll-controlled playback. The adapted footage remains under CC BY 3.0. No endorsement by the author or athletes is implied.

`motion.json` describes the frame rate and foreground bounds of this adaptation. The movement retains the fixed viewpoint of the original recording; it is not a freely rotating three-dimensional athlete model.

`judoka.png` is the existing project illustration used for the opening portrait.
